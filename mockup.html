A simpler Clay Journal

Same features, less on screen. Three rules: one stage at a time instead of nine open sections; numbers as plain rows instead of boxes and uppercase labels; and no panels — a single sheet of paper with hairlines.

Static mockup. The app itself is unchanged until you say go.
